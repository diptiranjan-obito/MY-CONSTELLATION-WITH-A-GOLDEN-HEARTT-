01 · you can put some of it down

What are you carrying tonight?

You don't have to explain it. Just choose whatever feels familiar. You can choose more than one.

You've been carrying a lot.

You don't have to carry all of it at once.
Nothing has to be solved in this moment. For now, let yourself breathe.
02 · if you could borrow my eyes

Things I wish you could see about yourself.

Not because I think you need to become someone else. Quite the opposite.

You care.

You notice things. You worry about people. Sometimes you care so much that you forget to leave some of that kindness for yourself.

You make people feel seen.

Being someone around whom another person can feel heard is not a small thing. It's a quiet kind of light.

You are allowed to be tired.

You don't have to be cheerful, useful, available, or okay every second to still matter.

You are still learning.

Making mistakes doesn't make your entire heart bad. You can learn, grow, apologize, and still have kindness for yourself.

Your worst moment isn't your biography.

One difficult conversation, one bad day, or one mistake doesn't get to write every chapter of who you are.

You don't have to earn your place.

You don't have to perform goodness perfectly for the people who genuinely care about you.

05 · before you go

You don't have to fix
your whole life tonight.

Drink a little water. Let your shoulders rest. Take one slow breath. Let tonight be just tonight.

I can't take every difficult thing away from you, and I won't pretend I can. But I can remind you that you don't have to face every difficult thing in silence.

You are allowed to be tired.
You are allowed to be confused.
You are allowed to have a bad day.
And you are still you.

I wish you could see yourself for one minute through the eyes of someone who cares about you.

Maybe then you'd understand why I call you

my constellation with a golden heart.

— someone who will keep reminding you 🤍
